# After the campaign: getting strong

**Summary.** The endgame changes more between patches than anything else, and version 1.0 (11 December 2026) overhauls it. Teach it from the current patch, not from memory. This file gives the order of work, what is already known for this character, and how to research the rest.

## Campaign-done checklist

Before the first map, check each item and give the exact fix for any that fails:

- Fire, cold and lightning resistance each at 75% after the endgame -60% penalty. The character sheet should already show the value after the penalty **[general]**.
- All 24 quest-book points and 100 quest Spirit taken (King in the Mists, Ignagduk, Lythara).
- 6 ascendancy points spent (Loyal Hellhound, Altered Flesh, Beidat's Will). Points 5-6 come from three floors of the Trial of the Sekhemas with the Djinn Barya that Elzarah, the Cobra Lord drops in the interludes **[checked: poe2db]**. Points 7-8 come at about level 80 (`campaign.md`).
- Movement speed boots (20% or more).
- Minion gems at the highest level his character level and Intelligence allow.
- Every sniper (or warrior) the Spirit pays for is summoned.

## Making the character strong, in order

1. **Don't die:** resistances capped, then life and energy shield. Altered Flesh already turns part of the cold and lightning damage he takes into fire.
2. **Minion skill levels:** each "+1 to Level of all Minion Skills" (sceptre, amulet) raises every minion gem **[checked text]**. The best value upgrade for a minion build **[opinion: Maxroll]**.
3. **More minions:** more Spirit = more snipers or warriors. Sources: quest Spirit (done), Beidat's Will (+1 Spirit per 25 maximum life), Spirit on amulet or body armour.
4. **Multipliers:** keep supports at their best tier (Feeding Frenzy, Minion Mastery, Brutality). "More" from supports beats more small "increased" nodes once he has many (`mechanics.md`).
5. **Switch to Domino Army** if he wants the zero-button style. Run the tests in `build.md` first. Use the plain army for lone bosses.

## Endgame structure: research it live

Before explaining maps, waystones, the atlas or bosses:

1. Read the current patch notes for endgame changes.
2. Find one or two current-patch endgame guides (Maxroll, Mobalytics, poe-vault, or the official site's endgame page). Note the patch each was written for.
3. Teach it in plain words: what a waystone is, how to open the first map, how map tiers rise, what to do with the first currency, and which bosses to try first.
4. Mark what comes from guides as opinion.

Things to cover once he is mapping:

- **Defensive targets** for the content he is in (resistances, life, chaos resistance). Get these from current guides and his own numbers, not from memory.
- **Crafting for his slots:** sceptre (+minion levels, Spirit), amulet (+minion levels, Spirit, resistances), boots (movement speed, resistances). Give the exact currency steps from current poe2db mod data.
- **Bosses:** on lone bosses, the plain army does 1.5 to 2.5 times more than the Domino loop **[hand, PoB2 numbers]**. Swap Infernal Legion for a damage support before a boss.
