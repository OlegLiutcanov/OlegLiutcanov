# Gear for the minion Witch (patch 0.5.5h)

**Summary.** For items, use the priority list below and answer in two lines: Equip or Skip, plus the one thing to watch. Marks: **[checked]** = poe2db or PoB2; **[opinion]** = guides; **[test]** = check in game.

## Priority while levelling (top wins ties)

1. **A sceptre that grants Skeletal Warrior.** Every base has 100 Spirit **[checked]**. Never be without one once the army depends on it. Prefer one that also has "+ to Level of all Minion Skills": +1 can roll from item level 2, +2 from 25 **[checked: poe2db]**. A sceptre with +minion levels beats a plain Rattling one **[opinion: Maxroll]**.
2. **Movement speed on boots:** 10% from item level 1, 15% from 16, 20% from 33 **[checked: poe2db]**. Guides call it the most important speed item **[opinion]**.
3. **Resistances toward the act's need** (cap 75%; each act from Act 2 takes 10% off; see `mechanics.md`). Cheap fixes **[checked: poe2db]**: Ruby (fire), Sapphire (cold) and Topaz (lightning) rings with +20-30% from level 8; a Lesser rune (Desert, Glacial or Storm: one per element) socketed in armour gives +10% to one resistance (drops from level 5); the normal rune gives +14% (needs level 15); resistance charms give +25% for 4 seconds when used.
4. **"+ to Level of all Minion Skills" on an amulet** (+1 from item level 5 **[checked]**). It raises the sniper and Raging Spirits gems. Whether it also raises the sceptre's own Skeletal Warrior is unconfirmed **[test: hover the skill before and after equipping]**.
5. **Spirit on other slots:** an amulet or Intelligence body armour can roll about +30 Spirit from item level 16 **[checked: poe2db]**. Each 30 to 40 Spirit is one more sniper.
6. **Life and energy shield.**
7. **Damage for himself** (wand or spell damage) matters least: the minions do the killing.

## How to judge a pasted item

- Read the item class, required level, required attributes, "Grants Skill" line and every mod.
- Can he wear it now (level and attributes)? If not, say "Skip for now; wear at level X".
- What does it change against what he wears? Count Spirit, + minion skill levels, movement speed, and each resistance. Compare with the cap and the act's penalty.
- **Swapping a sceptre:** the new one must also grant Skeletal Warrior (or he loses the warriors), and it should have the same or a higher skill level. Whether his support gems on the warrior skill carry over to the new sceptre is unconfirmed **[test: note the supports before swapping]**.
- Watch for lost resistances: an upgrade that drops a resistance below what he needs is a sidegrade. Say what fixes the gap.
- Intelligence lost on a swap can lower the level of a gem he uses (gem levels need Intelligence). Check the requirement table in `mechanics.md`.

**Answer template**

> **Equip.** Skill level 7 instead of 3 makes each extra warrior cost 23 Spirit instead of 33, and +1 minion level makes the snipers hit harder.
> Watch: you lose 12% fire resistance. Put a Ruby ring on, or socket a fire rune.

## Shopping habits

- Vendors restock when he levels up **[opinion]**. In the search box: `nion sk|movem` highlights minion skill levels and movement speed **[opinion]**.
- Gold: buy movement speed boots, + minion level sceptres and amulets, and resistance rings. Save about 20,000 gold for the Ferryman's one-time Greater Runes in Interlude 1 **[opinion: Maxroll]**.
- Sell or disenchant what he doesn't need **[opinion]**. Don't spend rare currency (Exalted, Divine) on levelling gear.
- Trade: the official trade site works in the trade leagues. A new account has no currency, so a few cheap upgrades (resistance rings, movement speed boots, a +2 minion sceptre) are the best value **[opinion]**.
